# Portfolio visual direction

Content reference: https://www.themuneebh.com/ (structure and narrative only; the visual design is our own).

- Paper: #eef0ec; stone surfaces: #e3e7df; ink: #191d24; accent: #1f6f5f (signal green, used only for interactive things and the timeline); secondary text: #5b6169; rules: #cdd2cb.
- Bricolage Grotesque via next/font, one family, medium-weight oversized headings, tracking no tighter than -0.04em.
- Desktop hero: introduction at left; cut-out cartoon portrait on an ink panel at right, head breaking above the panel edge. Mobile: single column.
- One accent glyph on the two primary calls to action only. No eyebrow labels except the timeline dates. No slogan fragments.
- Positioning: whole business processes automated with AI, a person involved only on exceptions. The handover rate is the metric. The Processes section lists process types he automates, not case studies; the Work section holds the shipped examples.
- The four-station rail (Intake, Decide, Act, Recover; where people get stuck / what I build) is the memorable element; everything else stays quiet.
- The results strip and per-project numbers are real figures from Hunain (Sept 2026). Track Loop is a prototype and must always say so.
- Home is a five-second pitch: hero, results, about, what I deliver, one featured case study (xAIa), testimonials, contact. Depth lives on /work (all case studies, code), /approach (rail, what I automate, research, stack, FAQ), /about (journey, recognition). Sections are exported from Portfolio.tsx and composed per route.
- Portrait is the focal motion: entrance, pointer perspective, native scroll depth. Honor reduced motion; no autoplay loops or scroll interception.
- Preserve factual career and project details; do not adopt the reference author's achievements or commercial claims.
